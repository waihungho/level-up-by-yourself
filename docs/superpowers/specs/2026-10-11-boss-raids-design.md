# Weekly Boss Raids — Design Spec (Sub-project 3 of 3)

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Draft — awaiting user review
**Depends on:** Tactical Battles (engine, sessions) and Squad Battles (squads, squad engine)

---

## Why

With about 14 daily players, PvP alone feels empty. A **shared weekly boss** makes a small playerbase feel like a community. Everyone chips away at one giant HP bar, sees each other's hits land, and celebrates the kill together. It's also the spectacle piece: a huge animated boss, a global HP bar and a kill cinematic, all good for demos and dApp Store screenshots.

**Success criteria (per week, first 4 weeks):**
- ≥10 unique raid participants per week (≈ most of the core)
- Boss defeated in 3 of 4 weeks (tuning works)
- Players who join raids show higher week-over-week retention than those who don't (compare cohorts)

---

## Player Experience

- **`/raid` page** (a new 🐉 nav item replaces "Guide", which moves into the dashboard menu):
  - A giant boss sprite (Pixi, using the existing bloom/glow filters), its name and lore line, its **weakness** and **resistance** categories, the global HP bar, a countdown to week end, and a live **hit feed** ("Kade's squad hit for 12,340 — COUNTER!").
  - A contribution leaderboard for this boss, with your rank highlighted.
  - "Attack" button: **3 raid attempts per player per day**.
- **An attempt** is a tactical squad battle (your saved squad, or the auto-squad) against the boss for **up to 10 rounds** or until your squad is wiped. The boss does not die inside one attempt. Your damage is subtracted from the global HP when the attempt ends. Players with fewer than 3 agents attack solo with their best agent, and their damage is ×1.5 so they still matter.
- Score = total damage dealt. A summary screen shows damage, best hit, counters, and rank movement.
- **Kill:** whoever's attempt drops the global HP to 0 gets the **Last Hit** bonus. Everyone sees a kill cinematic the next time they open `/raid` or the dashboard (shown once per boss).

## Boss Rules

- The boss is a special `SideState` with huge HP. Only damage dealt counts; the boss's HP within an attempt starts from the current global HP.
- **Intents** come from its category profile, with three **phases** set by *global* HP:
  - Phase 1 (100–66%): normal intents. Weakness ×1.5 damage, resistance ×0.5.
  - Phase 2 (66–33%): weakness rotates to a new category (announced in the feed). The boss telegraphs **ULTIMATE** every 4th round: unguarded agents take 3× damage.
  - Phase 3 (<33%): Enrage. Boss damage +30%, ULTIMATE every 3rd round, and counters deal ×1.75 instead of ×1.5 (high risk, high reward).
- The boss never feints (it should be readable) and is never stunned (Shield Bash gives +50% damage instead).
- Boss stats scale with the median core agent so fights stay dangerous: `catAvg_boss(C) = p75 of active agents' catAvg(C) × profileMultiplier(C)`, computed when the boss spawns.

### Boss roster

Five hand-written bosses rotate weekly. Each has a name, lore, category profile, starting weakness/resistance, phase-2 weakness, and sprite seed:

| Boss | Profile | Weak → P2 Weak | Resist |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Colossus | Physical/Technical heavy | Social → Mental | Physical |
| Mind Leviathan | Mental | Technical → Social | Mental |
| Hollow Choir | Social | Spiritual → Physical | Social |
| Void Oracle | Spiritual | Mental → Technical | Spiritual |
| Rogue Singularity | Technical | Physical → Spiritual | Technical |

The roster lives in `src/lib/raid/bosses.ts`, and sprites are generated by the existing `sprite-renderer` at 3× scale with a boss palette.

### Weekly lifecycle and HP tuning

- A new Vercel cron, `/api/cron/raid-weekly`, runs Monday 00:00 UTC (protected by `CRON_SECRET` like daily-growth). It:
  1. Closes the current boss: `expired` if alive, and pays out undefeated rewards at 50%.
  2. Spawns the next boss in rotation with `max_hp = clamp(lastWeekTotalDamage × 1.1, MIN_HP, MAX_HP)`. If last week's boss died on day D, the multiplier is `1.1 × (5 / max(D, 1))`, capped at 1.6. The target is a kill around Friday. Week 1 uses an estimate: `14 players × 3 attempts × 5 days × median attempt damage (from simulation)`.
- The cron is idempotent: it keys on `week_start`, so re-running it is safe.

### Rewards (paid when the boss dies or the week expires)

| Tier | Who | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Champion | #1 damage | +300 ability score, Champion trophy, +3.0 growth spread over the boss's weakness-category dims for each squad agent |
| Elite | #2–3 | +150 ability, Elite trophy, +2.0 growth |
| Vanguard | top 10 | +75 ability, Vanguard trophy, +1.0 growth |
| Participant | ≥1 attempt | +30 ability, +0.5 growth |
| Last Hit | killing blow | +100 ability, Slayer trophy (stacks with tier) |

Growth goes to the squad saved at payout time (or auto-squad). Ability uses the existing `increment_ability_score`. Expired bosses pay 50% and no Slayer. Trophies show on the rank page and the agent/player profile.

Raid attempts also give normal battle growth to the participating agents (×0.6, the same as squad), so raids replace PvP time instead of adding a separate chore.

`payoutBoss` is guarded by `paid_out_at IS NULL` (via `UPDATE ... WHERE paid_out_at IS NULL RETURNING`), so payout happens exactly once even if the kill and the cron race.

An attempt that finishes after the boss died (another player killed it mid-attempt) still records contribution and growth, but `killed` is false. That damage counts for leaderboard placement until payout runs, and payout includes it if it lands before `paid_out_at` is set.

### Live updates

`/raid` subscribes via Supabase Realtime to `UPDATE` on `levelup_raid_bosses` (HP bar) and `INSERT` on `levelup_raid_hits` (feed). If the realtime channel errors, it falls back to polling every 15s. Realtime must be enabled for these two tables in the Supabase dashboard (deployment step).

### Error handling

- `no_active_boss` (409) between expiry and spawn, or if the cron failed. The dashboard banner hides itself, and the cron route logs and returns 500 so Vercel shows the failure.
- `raid_limit_reached` (429).
- The damage RPC failing after the attempt finishes: the session stays `active` with `finalize_pending`, and the next start/turn call retries finalisation before doing anything else.

### Testing

- `raid-engine.test.ts`: phase transitions by global HP, ULTIMATE cadence per phase, weakness/resistance multipliers, 10-round cap, solo ×1.5.
- `raid-db.test.ts`: concurrent damage → exactly one `killed = true`; payout idempotent; contributions sum equals hp dealt.
- `raid-cron.test.ts`: idempotent spawn, HP tuning formula, expired 50% payout.
- Manual: two devices attacking at once, checking the HP bar and feed update on both.

### Out of scope

On-chain trophies/NFTs (a possible later add-on using the existing Solana stack), paid extra attempts, guilds, boss-specific mechanics beyond phases/ULTIMATE.

## Rollout order (all three sub-projects)

1. Tactical Battles: replaces the current training and PvP fights. Ship, then watch `manual_actions` for 1 week.
2. Squad Battles: adds the squad builder + squad PvP.
3. Boss Raids: launch on a Monday, with a dashboard announcement banner.

Each step is a web deploy only. The Android app is a WebView, so no APK or dApp Store resubmission is needed.
